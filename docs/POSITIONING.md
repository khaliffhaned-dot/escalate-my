## What it is

Escalate MY is a Crisis Intelligence Platform designed to help organisations navigate critical incidents through disciplined governance, structured workflows and executive decision support.

Unlike traditional communications software, Escalate MY focuses on **the decisions behind the response**.

The platform provides a common operating picture for leadership teams, legal advisors, corporate affairs professionals, operational stakeholders and crisis committees.

Built for listed companies, government-linked organisations, regulated industries and enterprise leadership teams.
